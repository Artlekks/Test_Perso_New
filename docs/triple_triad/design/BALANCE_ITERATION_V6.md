# Triple Triad Balance Iteration V6

## What V5 proved

The neutral benchmark separated raw deck/AI power from authored home rules.

V5 neutral adjacent results:
- Dock over Beach: 91.9% decisive wins — too steep.
- Highland over Dock: 76.6% — barely above target.
- Tide over Highland: 69.1% — healthy.
- Ash over Tide: 100% — not a valid normal rung because Rank 5 was missing.

## V6 progression changes

### Rank 1 — Beach Trader
Preferred deck moves to a 72-rank-total / 26-point configuration:
Mina, Saltbeard, Swiftfin, Gimbal, Harbor Guard.
Gimbal preserves the beginner Influence lesson.

### Rank 2 — Dock Bruiser
Unchanged. V6 uses Beach as the tuning side of this gap rather than destabilizing
Dock again.

### Rank 3 — Highland Keeper
Small trim: Cave Bear is replaced by Glow Ram. This reduces printed total by two
and deck cost by one while preserving the defensive profile.

### Rank 4 — Tide Oracle
Unchanged. Its V5 neutral result against Highland was already on target.

### Rank 5 — Storm Captain
New Same + Combo specialist using Trickster AI.
Preferred deck:
Veyra, Korr, Teo, Jax, Rena.
Deck cost 38; printed rank total 110.

### Rank 6 — Ash Champion
Now requires player Duel Rank 5 and awards 30 progression points.
