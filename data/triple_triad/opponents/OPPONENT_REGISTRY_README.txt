Triple Triad card-player ecosystem - V1

Progression spine (used by balance simulator):
1. Beach Trader      - beginner / Influence introduction
2. Dock Bruiser      - aggressive capture pressure
3. Highland Keeper   - defensive / protected-side play
4. Tide Oracle       - Plus + Influence control
5. Storm Captain     - Same + Combo specialist
6. Ash Champion      - endgame capstone; requires Storm Captain first

Side/world card players:
- Pier Apprentice    - Rank 1 casual beginner
- Gearwright         - Rank 2 construct/defensive
- Marsh Keeper       - Rank 3 beast/defensive
- Lantern Gambler    - Rank 4 Plus/Influence trickster
- Wandering Sage     - Rank 5 Same/Combo traveler

Progression points are first-clear rewards by default. Rematches remain useful
for card stakes/recovery but do not farm Duel Points unless a profile explicitly
authors a rematch reward.

New saves begin with zero cards. The salvaged_card_case acquisition bundle grants
the ten starter cards and unlocks Triple Triad. Existing saves with cards migrate
as already unlocked.
